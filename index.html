How it works

Four simple steps.

1

Register

Open Register, choose Tourist, Tour Guide or Agency Manager, and fill in the details that type needs.

2

Book a tour

Pick a package and a travel date. The agency manager checks the seats and approves it.

3

Pay

Pay online after approval. A guide and a vehicle are assigned to your tour.

4

Travel and rate

Enjoy the tour, then rate the package and your guide.
